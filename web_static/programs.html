Beginner

Our beginner classes offer a welcoming, supportive introduction to martial arts for all ages and fitness levels. Students learn fundamental techniques, basic strikes, blocks, footwork, and self-defense maneuvers, all in a safe and friendly environment. Classes focus on building confidence, coordination, discipline, and physical fitness, while progressing at a comfortable pace tailored to each individual. Whether your goal is self-defense, fitness, or personal growth, our beginner classes provide a fun and motivating way to start your martial arts journey.

Fitness Drills

These typically include basic stances and footwork, bodyweight exercises like push-ups and squats, striking combinations with punches and kicks, and movement patterns such as pivots and shadowboxing. The goal is to build foundational fitness and technique in a fun, accessible way that prepares students for effective martial arts practice while boosting overall health and confidence.

Self Defense

Focuses on practical moves such as escaping grabs, delivering quick strikes with hands or elbows, using your body’s natural weapons like knees, and maintaining awareness to avoid danger. The goal is to create opportunities to escape safely and avoid injury by using leverage, timing, and basic strikes rather than relying on strength alone.

Taekwondo

Known for its high, fast kicks and dynamic footwork, Taekwondo combines combat techniques, self-defense, sport, and exercise, emphasizing discipline, respect, and mental focus. Classes available for all those with a passion to fly high.

Karate

Karate training includes practicing forms (kata), sparring, and conditioning to build strength, agility, and focus. It combines powerful punches, kicks, strikes, blocks, and throws with mental discipline, respect, and personal development.
